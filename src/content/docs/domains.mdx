<GuideCallout title="Note" variant="info">
  Domains help in organising and governing data across the platform. Assigning a domain to data assets, projects, or glossary terms ensures consistent classification and discoverability.
</GuideCallout>

<GuideCallout title="Note" variant="info">
  Linked projects and assets will appear in the Usage section once the domain is assigned and in active use. Usage counts update automatically as assets are linked.
</GuideCallout>

<GuideCallout title="Note" variant="info">
  The Color field is used as a visual indicator in the UI — it appears on domain chips and badges across the platform, making domains instantly recognisable at a glance.
</GuideCallout>
