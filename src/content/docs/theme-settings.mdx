<GuideCallout title="Note" variant="note">
  <ul>
    <li>Changes to Theme Settings apply platform-wide immediately after saving.</li>
    <li>Uploaded logos must be SVG or PNG with a transparent background for best results.</li>
    <li>Color seeds are used to generate full tonal palettes — avoid very dark or very light seed values.</li>
    <li>The Live Preview reflects the current unsaved state; publish to apply changes to all users.</li>
  </ul>
</GuideCallout>
